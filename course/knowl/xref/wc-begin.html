Writing convention 10. Begin every sentence with a word, not a symbol.

This avoids problems with whether you should capitalize a variable or not. Symbols also make it harder to tell where a sentence begins.
🔗
Good example: “Because \(n = 2k\text{,}\) by definition we know that \(n\) is even.”
🔗
Bad example: “\(n = 2k\text{,}\) so by definition we know that \(n\) is even.”
