Writing convention 12. Display (center) important equations and align “\(=\)” signs.

This means putting important equations, centered, on their own line. Only center important equations that are key to the proof or its conclusion. Minor or “helper” equations can be left inline. If the left side of the equation does not change, don’t repeat it. For example:
🔗
\begin{align*} x \cdot y &= (2m+1)(2n+1)\\ & = 4mn + 2m + 2n + 1\\ & = 2(2mn+m+n) + 1. \end{align*}
