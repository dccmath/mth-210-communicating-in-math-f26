Writing convention 17. Include a summary at the end of the proof and an “end of proof” symbol.

It’s considered good form to sum up the proof once you’ve finished the main writing, and to clearly show the reader where the proof ends. Briefly state what you actually proved, and put a box at the end of that line. This can be summarized or simplified compared to the original theorem statement, as long as it is still true.
